TRAPSHIFT — Privacy Policy

Nothing is what it looks like. But your privacy is exactly what it looks like — respected.
Last updated: June 11, 2026

The short version

TrapShift is a fully offline game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, and no third-party trackers. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

TrapShift does not collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Location data (precise or approximate)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Contacts, photos, files, or media
  • Health, financial, or biometric information
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Messages or any user-generated content

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To save your game progress, TrapShift stores a small amount of data only on your own device using your browser's / app's local storage. This includes:

  • Which levels you have completed and your best times / star ratings
  • Collected in-game shards and unlocked skins
  • Your daily-login streak counter
  • Your settings (music on/off, sound on/off, volume, touch size, etc.)

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server, and we have no access to it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage / site data or uninstalling the app.

3. No Accounts & No Sign-In

TrapShift does not require or support any form of account creation, login, or registration. You can play the entire game anonymously.

4. No Advertisements & No Tracking

This game contains no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We do not use cookies for tracking, and we do not share anything with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Device Permissions

TrapShift may request the following device features, purely to enable gameplay:

  • Vibration (haptics) — used only to provide tactile feedback (for example, a short buzz on a jump or a stronger pulse on a death). It can be reduced by lowering sound/haptics and is never used to identify you.
  • Audio — used to play the game's procedural music and sound effects. No microphone access is requested or used.

TrapShift does not request access to your camera, contacts, storage files, or precise location.

6. Children's Privacy

TrapShift does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your region). Because the game collects no personal data at all, it is safe for general audiences. The game is rated for everyone.

7. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no server-side data to secure or breach. Your game progress lives only on your device, under your control.

8. Third-Party Services

TrapShift does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social-media services. The game runs entirely offline after it is installed.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how TrapShift handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Because the game collects no personal data, we do not expect material changes. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the updated policy.
